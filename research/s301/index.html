Orbit, 2014–2041

Sky track of S301 for a non-spinning (χ = 0) and a maximally spinning (χ = 1, ξ = 0°) Sgr A*. The frames are uniform in observed time, so the pericentre passage really is this fast. The two tracks separate only in the inset and in the bottom panel, which shows the astrometric difference in μas against the GRAVITY+ precision.
